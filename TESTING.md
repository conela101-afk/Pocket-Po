# Testing on your phone

Everything below was tested by Claude in a desktop browser that pretends to be a phone. That cannot cover a real home screen install, a real touchscreen, real sound or vibration, or how the app feels in your hand. These are the checks that need you. Nothing here needs any personal detail, and please don't put any in the repo.

**One rule for all of it:** if something is hard, confusing or unpleasant to use, that is the finding. You don't need to work out why.

## Before you start
1. Open the app's web address once with a connection, and leave it a few seconds.
2. Add it to the home screen (see `README.md`).
3. Open it from the home screen icon, not from the browser.

## Both phones
- [ ] It opens full screen with no browser bars.
- [ ] The icon looks right on the home screen.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode, close the app fully, open it again. It still works.
- [ ] Press and hold the icon (Android) or use the Now shortcut if offered. It goes straight to Now.
- [ ] From a cold start, Now is one tap. The cards appear at once.
- [ ] Every button is easy to hit with a thumb. Nothing is too small.
- [ ] Text is readable at arm's length. Try the text size setting.
- [ ] Dark mode looks comfortable, and so does light mode. Try the theme setting.
- [ ] Reduce motion (in Settings, and in the phone's own settings) stops Po's movement and the breathing ring sweep. The words In and Out still show.
- [ ] No sound plays unless you turned sound on.

## Tools worth trying by hand
- [ ] Breathing: the pace feels slow enough. Try the comfort toggle.
- [ ] Cool water screens: the safety note shows once before first use, and again from the **i** button.
- [ ] Pet Po and Pattern trace: they follow your finger smoothly and don't scroll the page.
- [ ] Bubble pop and Sort shapes: taps land where you touch.
- [ ] Type in a list and a plan with the on-screen keyboard. Check that the keyboard doesn't hide the Back button or what you're typing.
- [ ] The after-tool row: is 8 seconds a good time before it fades? Is it easy to tap? Do Quieter and Louder make sense as the ends of the scale?
- [ ] Handoff: the button opens your messaging app with the message and no recipient.

## Export and backup
- [ ] Appointment summary: Print or save as PDF works from the installed app. Check it fits one page.
- [ ] Detailed report: two pages at most.
- [ ] CSV: saves, and opens in a spreadsheet app.
- [ ] Backup file: saves somewhere you can find it.
- [ ] Restore that backup. Everything is back.
- [ ] Best test of all: restore the backup on a second device, if you have one.

## Updates
- [ ] After Claude changes the app, close it fully and open it twice. The second time it should be the new version, with no message and no interruption.

## Screen reader (optional)
- [ ] With VoiceOver (iPhone) or TalkBack (Android), Home, Now and one tool can be used. Buttons say what they do.

## What to tell Claude
Anything that felt hard, slow, wrong or surprising, in your own words. One line is enough.
